  [1X2.3 [33X[0;0YInflation of Lie algebras[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YA  grading  for a Lie algebra [23XL[123X is a decomposition L = [23X\bigoplus_{i=1}^n L_i[123X
  that  respects  the  Lie  bracket,  [13Xi.e.[113X  [23X[L_i,L_j]\subseteq  L_{i+j}[123X.}. Any
  nilpotent    Lie    algebras    can    be    graded    by   taking   [23XL_i   =
  \gamma_i(L)/\gamma_{i+1}(L)[123X.  Let  [23XL[123X  be  a nilpotent Lie algebra of maximal
  class.  The  two-step centralizers are the sets [23XC_i = C_{L_1}(L_i) = \{x \in
  L_1    \mid   [x,L_i]   =   0   \}[123X   for   all   [23X2\leq   i   \leq   c[123X.   Let
  [23X\mathcal{C}=\{C_i\}\setminus L_1[123X, we say that a Lie algebra of maximal class
  is  covered  if the set [23X\mathcal{C}[123X consist of all one-dimensional subspaces
  of [23XL_1[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet [23XL[123X be a graded Lie algebra [23XL = \bigoplus_{i=1}^n L_i[123X over [23XK=\F_q[123X for some
  prime  power  [23Xq=p^n[123X.  Consider  the field extension [23XA=K[\varepsilon]/\langle
  \varepsilon^p  \rangle[123X  which  is  a vector space over [23XK[123X of dimension [23Xp[123X. The
  algebra  [23XA[123X  is  an  associative,  commutative  and  has  a unit. The algebra
  [23XL\otimes A[123X over [23XK[123X defined by [23X[x\otimes a, y\otimes b] = [x,y]\otimes ab[123X is a
  graded  Lie  algebra.  Let  [23XM[123X  be  a maximal ideal of [23XL[123X and consider the Lie
  subalgebra  [23XM^\uparrow = M\otimes A[123X. Let [23XD[123X be a derivation of [23XL[123X of degree 1.
  Define   [23XD^\uparrow[123X   via:   [23XD^\uparrow(x\otimes  \varepsilon^i)=D(x)\otimes
  \varepsilon^i[123X,  this  is  a derivation of [23XM^\uparrow[123X of degree 1. Define the
  derivation   [23XE\in   \Der   M^\uparrow[123X   by   [23XE(x\otimes   \varepsilon^i)   =
  D^\uparrow(x\otimes\varepsilon^i\cdot\varepsilon^{p-1})+1\otimes\partial_\varepsilon(\varepsilon^i)[123X.
  Let  [23Xs\in  L_1\setminus  M[123X, take [23XD=\mathrm{ad}_s[123X, and extend it naturally to
  [23XM^\uparrow[123X. Denote [23XE_{s^\prime}[123X as previously. The [13Xinflation[113X [23X{}^{M}L[123X of [23XL[123X at
  [23XM[123X  by [23Xs\in L_1\setminus M[123X is the graded Lie algebra obtained as an extension
  of  [23XM^\uparrow[123X  by  an  element  [23Xs^\prime[123X,  which is the extension of [23Xs[123X that
  induces the derivation [23XE_{s^\prime}[123X, that is,[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y[x\otimes      a,     s^\prime]     =     E_{s^\prime}(x\otimes     a)     =
  \mathrm{ad}_{s^\prime}(x\otimes\varepsilon^i)+x\otimes\partial_\varepsilon(\varepsilon^i).[133X
