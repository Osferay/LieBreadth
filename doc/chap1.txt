  [1X1.1 [33X[0;0YIntroduction[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YGiven a Lie algebra [23XL[123X, we define its lower central series as [23XL = L^1 > L^2 >
  \dots[123X,  where  [23XL^{i+1} = L^i L[123X. The algebra [23XL[123X is nilpotent if there exists [23Xc
  \in  \N[123X  such that [23XL^{c+1} = {0}[123X, and the minimal such [23Xc[123X is called the class
  [23X\mathrm{cl}(L)[123X  of  [23XL[123X.  For  a nilpotent Lie algebra [23XL[123X, the type of [23XL[123X is the
  vector  [23X(d_1,\dots,d_c)[123X,  where [23Xd_i = \dim L^i[123X. A nilpotent Lie algebra [23XL[123X is
  said  to  be  of  maximal  class  if  the  type  of  [23XL[123X is [23X(2,1,\dots,1)[123X. The
  centralizer  of  [23Xx  \in L[123X is the subspace of [23XL[123X defined by [23XC_L(x) = \{a \in L

  [24X[33X[0;6Y\mathrm{br}(L)  = \max \{ \mathrm{br}(x) \mid x \in L\}, \;\; \mbox{ where }

  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe    class-breadth    conjecture    asserts   that   [23X\mathrm{cl}(L)   \leq
  \mathrm{br}(L)+1[123X  for  an  algebra  [23XL[123X. This holds for nilpotent Lie algebras
  over  infinite  fields and for nilpotent associative algebras over arbitrary
  fields.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet [23XL[123X be a Lie algebra over a field [23XK[123X, and let [23XB = \{ b_1, \ldots, b_n \}[123X be

  [124X

  matrices  of [23XL[123X. For [23Xx = x_1 b_1 + \ldots + x_n b_n \in L[123X, let [23X\overline{x} =

  \times  n[123X  matrix over [23XK[123X whose [23Xk[123X-th column is [23XC_k \overline{x}^{tr}[123X, this is
